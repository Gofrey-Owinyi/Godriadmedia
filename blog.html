What Is GodriadMedia? Kenya's Free Global Social Media App

GodriadMedia is a free global social media app built in Kenya by Godfrey Owinyi. You can post photos, watch Godriad Reels, chat privately, follow friends, join groups, video call, and watch Real TV — all in one place.

What you can do in GodriadMedia

  • Post up to 40 photos per account, with up to 3 photos per post
  • Watch and upload Godriad Reels (20 per user)
  • Private 1-to-1 chats (80 chats per user) — no public chats, no self-chat
  • Video and voice calls with ringtone, vibration and missed-call alerts
  • Boost your post, get VIP, send Live Gifts, buy data bundles
  • Watch Real TV: Citizen, NTV, KTN via YouTube Live

One phone number can create up to 2 Normal accounts and 1 Business account. You can switch accounts on the front page, and you can make your account Private.

Godriad Reels: How To Upload, Like And Grow

Godriad Reels are short videos inside the app. You can upload up to 20 reels per account. When you reach 20 and upload one more, the oldest reel is automatically deleted — you are never blocked from posting.

How to upload a Reel

  • Tap Reels at the bottom
  • Tap + Upload
  • Pick a video from your phone
  • Your reel appears in the Reels feed for everyone to like

Likes are counted per person — one like per person, no fake likes.

GodriadMedia FAQ — Limits, Payments, Search & Install

Will GodriadMedia show in Google?

Yes. This blog is static HTML so Google can read every article. Submit godriadmedia.vercel.app/blog.html in Google Search Console → Request Indexing → Wait 1-7 days → Search “GodriadMedia” or “GodriadMedia Blog”.

Does it work in Chrome?

Yes. Open godriadmedia.vercel.app in Chrome. Tap Install to add it to your home screen.

What if I reach my post limit?

You are NOT blocked. Post #41 deletes Post #1 (oldest) and keeps #2 to #41. Same for chats (80) and reels (20).

Why did Paystack say “Currency not supported”?

Because the app tried to charge in USD. Your Paystack account only has KES. Now the app always charges in KES. Your bank converts USD/EUR/etc to KES automatically.

Is my chat private?

Yes. Only sender and recipient can see it.

How do I get help?

Open the app → Money / People / Help, or search this blog for your topic.
