GodriadMedia FAQ — Limits, Payments, Search & Install

Will GodriadMedia show in Google?

Yes. This blog is static HTML so Google can read every article. Submit godriadmedia.vercel.app/blog.html in Google Search Console → Request Indexing → Wait 1-7 days → Search “GodriadMedia” or “GodriadMedia Blog”.

Does it work in Chrome?

Yes. Open godriadmedia.vercel.app in Chrome. Tap Install to add it to your home screen.

What if I reach my post limit?

You are NOT blocked. Post #41 deletes Post #1 (oldest) and keeps #2 to #41. Same for chats (80) and reels (20).

Why did Paystack say “Currency not supported”?

Because the app tried to charge in USD. Your Paystack account only has KES. Now the app always charges in KES. Your bank converts USD/EUR/etc to KES automatically.

Is my chat private?

Yes. Only sender and recipient can see it.

How do I get help?

Open the app → Money / People / Help, or search this blog for your topic.

GodriadMedia vs TikTok vs Facebook — Which Is Best For Kenyans?

All three are good. But they are NOT the same. Here is the honest comparison:

GodriadMedia — Best for Kenyans who want to sell + be seen fast

  • ✅ Boost KSh 200 for 7 days — cheap, M-Pesa works
  • ✅ VIP KSh 200/month — gold badge, stand out
  • ✅ Live Gifts 50/50 — Rose KSh 20, Diamond KSh 100, Crown KSh 500
  • ✅ Private chats, calls, Real TV (Citizen, NTV, KTN) in one app
  • ✅ Data bundles from KSh 5, Real TV KSh 100
  • ✅ Small community — your post is NOT buried by 1 million posts
  • ❌ Still growing — fewer users than TikTok / Facebook (for now)

TikTok — Best for viral videos

  • ✅ Huge audience, can go viral in 1 night
  • ✅ Good for dancers, comedy, music
  • ❌ Hard to sell — people watch, they don't buy
  • ❌ Boost is expensive, no M-Pesa, hard to get paid in Kenya
  • ❌ Your video can disappear in the feed in 1 hour

Facebook — Best for old customers + groups

  • ✅ Many Kenyans already there, good for Groups
  • ❌ Boost is expensive (KSh 1,000+), hard to target Eldoret
  • ❌ Your post is buried — only 5% of your friends see it unless you pay a lot
  • ❌ No Live Gifts 50/50, no Real TV in same app

So which should you use?

Use ALL three. Post on TikTok for viral, post on Facebook for old friends, post on GodriadMedia to SELL — because Boost is cheap, M-Pesa works, and your post stays at the top for 7 days.

Smart creators do this: Make 1 video → Post it on TikTok + Facebook + GodriadMedia Reels. Put in caption: “Buy on GodriadMedia — link in bio”.
